How numbers get marked

  • 41 million Nope Official data says otherwise.
  • 41 million Checks out Matches the official figure.
  • 41 million Double-check Only a forecast to compare with, or a census count.
  • 41 million Can't check No official figure for that year.

    How it checks

    1. Finds every number with rules, not AI. So nothing gets invented along the way: every number it checks is really in your text.
    2. Looks up the official figure. Populations come from the World Bank and the UN's World Population Prospects 2024. Conversions are calculated from exact unit definitions.
    3. Marks it, and shows the receipt. Every note names its source, so you can check Hallucinope too. It can be wrong, especially with unusual wording.

    It checks populations of countries and the world, and conversions of length, weight, temperature, speed, area and volume. Everything else is listed as not checked. It runs on your computer and sends your text nowhere.

    Hallucinope, a student project. Data: World Bank World Development Indicators; United Nations, World Population Prospects 2024.
